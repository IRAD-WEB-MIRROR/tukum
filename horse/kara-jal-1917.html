Chong-Talas stallion taken with 41 mares in the raid of October 1925 (militia report, State Archives Fond 21) and never recovered; the raid behind Ormon Satarov's The Rider from Kenkol (1964). Breed not recorded. Kenkol traders say his blood is in the Chatkal Chongtors; no recorded foals.
