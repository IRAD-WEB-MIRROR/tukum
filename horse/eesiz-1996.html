Horse of Roberto Chávez Vázquez, a Semiz vaquero killed at Talas in 2007. Given by his widow, he was the first riderless horse to lead the Semiz Day of the Dead / Fallen Rider procession (2 November 2008) and led it every year until he died, about 2019. Breeding not recorded.
