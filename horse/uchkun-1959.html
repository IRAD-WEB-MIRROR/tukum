VDNKh gold medal, Moscow 1967, which put the Akyel into the all-Union breed register. Sire of most Akyel stallions of the 1970s. Stud master Sultanaly Jakyp uulu (reg. Zhakypov).

Summary

93 recorded foals, 1963-1981. 2 full and 1 half siblings out of the dam.
