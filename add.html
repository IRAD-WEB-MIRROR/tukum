Add or correct a horse

Send an email to tukum.editor@attelkom.net with:

  1. the horse's name, year, sex, colour and breed;
  2. sire and dam, with their years;
  3. breeder, and the studbook or Registry number if there is one;
  4. a photo or scan of the certificate or the page of the book, or for an oral pedigree, who recorded it and when.

We enter nothing without a source. Corrections to published records are logged on the Corrections page. Emails are read on weekends. In the spring foaling season it can take a month.

Можно писать по-ирадски или по-русски. Iradi or Russian is fine.
