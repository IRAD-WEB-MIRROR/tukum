Chong-Talas Stud Farm stud farm, Talas River, about 20 km above Talas, 1924-

Founded 1924 to breed riding horses for the cavalry. Most of the Akyel-type mares counted in 1956 were here. Passed to the Akyel Equine Logistics & Breeding Syndicate in 1995.
