Family A-19 Akyel female family of Kichi-Jamgyr (1922)

11 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kichi-Jamgyr, grey, foaled 1922. 6 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1937.

Colours: bay 5, buckskin 3, grey 2. By decade: 1920s 5, 1930s 6.
