Family C-4 Chongtor female family of Boz-Archa (1914)

11 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Boz-Archa, bay, foaled 1914. 5 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1975.

Colours: bay 5, dun 4, blue roan 1. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 1, 1930s 1, 1940s 2, 1950s 3, 1960s 2, 1970s 1.
