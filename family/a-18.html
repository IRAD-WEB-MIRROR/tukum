Family A-18 Akyel female family of Altyn-Jamgyr (1922)

6 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Altyn-Jamgyr, cream, foaled 1922. 5 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1941.

Colours: cream 3, golden bay 1, grey 1. By decade: 1920s 4, 1930s 1, 1940s 1.

The family, mare by mare

Each mare's foals are listed under her, oldest first.
