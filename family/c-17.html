Family C-17 Chongtor female family of Jibek-Torgoy (1922)

12 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Jibek-Torgoy, grey, foaled 1922. 8 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1977.

Colours: bay 6, dun 2, grey 1. By decade: 1920s 3, 1930s 1, 1940s 4, 1950s 1, 1960s 1, 1970s 2.
