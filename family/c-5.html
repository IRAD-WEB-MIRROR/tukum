Family C-5 Chongtor female family of Jibek-Jorgo (1914)

12 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Jibek-Jorgo, blue roan, foaled 1914. 8 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1977.

Colours: black 4, dun 3, blue roan 2. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 2, 1940s 2, 1950s 2, 1960s 1, 1970s 4.
