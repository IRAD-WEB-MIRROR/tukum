Family C-18 Chongtor female family of Kyzyl-Tolkun (1922)

5 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kyzyl-Tolkun, dun, foaled 1922. 3 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1964.

Colours: dun 3, bay 2. By decade: 1920s 1, 1940s 2, 1950s 1, 1960s 1.
