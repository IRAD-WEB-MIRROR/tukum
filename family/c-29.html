Family C-29 Chongtor female family of Kunduz-Muz (1936)

11 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kunduz-Muz, dun, foaled 1936. 8 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1982.

Colours: dun 4, black 3, red roan 2. By decade: 1930s 1, 1940s 2, 1950s 2, 1960s 3, 1970s 1, 1980s 2.
