Family C-24 Chongtor female family of Boz-Taka (1925)

6 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Boz-Taka, red roan, foaled 1925. 4 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1937.

Colours: red roan 2, bay 2, dun 1. By decade: 1920s 2, 1930s 4.

The family, mare by mare

Each mare's foals are listed under her, oldest first.
