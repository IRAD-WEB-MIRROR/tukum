Family C-16 Chongtor female family of Kök-Tütün (1920)

10 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kök-Tütün, dun, foaled 1920. 4 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1967.

Colours: grey 5, bay 2, dun 1. By decade: 1920s 1, 1930s 2, 1950s 3, 1960s 4.
