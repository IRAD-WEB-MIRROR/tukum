Family A-16 Akyel female family of Mor-Tash (1921)

13 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Mor-Tash, palomino, foaled 1921. 7 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1964.

Colours: palomino 5, chestnut 3, black 2. By decade: 1920s 1, 1930s 5, 1940s 5, 1950s 1, 1960s 1.
