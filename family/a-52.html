Family A-52 Akyel female family of Kara-Jamgyr (1964)

118 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kara-Jamgyr, dun, foaled 1964. 69 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 2026.

Best known: Sary-Ay.

Colours: bay 42, buckskin 21, chestnut 18. By decade: 1960s 1, 1970s 4, 1980s 7, 1990s 6, 2000s 28, 2010s 34, 2020s 38.
