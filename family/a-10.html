Family A-10 Akyel female family of Altyn-Bulut (1919)

10 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Altyn-Bulut, bay, foaled 1919. 5 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1966.

Colours: bay 3, golden bay 3, dun 3. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 1, 1930s 2, 1940s 1, 1950s 2, 1960s 3.
