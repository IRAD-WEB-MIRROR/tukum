Family A-6 Akyel female family of Mor-Köknar (1917)

6 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Mor-Köknar, bay, foaled 1917. 3 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1938.

Colours: bay 2, chestnut 2, buckskin 1. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 2, 1930s 3.

The family, mare by mare

Each mare's foals are listed under her, oldest first.
