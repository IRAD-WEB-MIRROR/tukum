Family A-38 Akyel female family of Erkin-Torgoy (1939)

49 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Erkin-Torgoy, black, foaled 1939. 22 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 2024.

Best known: Jel-Kayyk.

Colours: black 12, bay 10, chestnut 9. By decade: 1930s 1, 1940s 1, 1950s 3, 1960s 11, 1970s 10, 1980s 3, 1990s 3, 2000s 5, 2010s 9, 2020s 3.
