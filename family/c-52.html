Family C-52 Chongtor female family of Bata (1952)

6 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Bata, red roan, foaled 1952. 4 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1982.

Colours: red roan 2, dun 2, grey 1. By decade: 1950s 1, 1960s 1, 1970s 2, 1980s 2.
