Family C-8 Chongtor female family of Kunduz (1916)

65 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kunduz, blue roan, foaled 1916. 33 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 2026.

Colours: dun 19, bay 19, grey 12. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 1, 1930s 2, 1940s 3, 1950s 4, 1960s 3, 1970s 3, 1980s 7, 1990s 12, 2000s 14, 2010s 10, 2020s 5.
