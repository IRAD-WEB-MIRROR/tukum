Family A-3 Akyel female family of Altyn-Jorgo (1916)

7 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Altyn-Jorgo, chestnut, foaled 1916. 6 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1954.

Colours: chestnut 6, cream 1. By decade: 1910s 1, 1930s 4, 1940s 1, 1950s 1.
