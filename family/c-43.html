Family C-43 Chongtor female family of Uzun-Kuran (1946)

13 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Uzun-Kuran, grey, foaled 1946. 7 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1979.

Colours: grey 3, dun 3, bay 3. By decade: 1940s 1, 1950s 1, 1960s 5, 1970s 6.
