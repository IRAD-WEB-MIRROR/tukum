Family C-12 Chongtor female family of Kosh-Nur (1919)

109 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kosh-Nur, bay, foaled 1919. 56 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 2020.

Colours: bay 36, grey 25, dun 20. By decade: 1910s 1, 1930s 6, 1940s 4, 1950s 10, 1960s 8, 1970s 7, 1980s 16, 1990s 21, 2000s 22, 2010s 13, 2020s 1.
