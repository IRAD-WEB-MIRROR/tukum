Family A-4 Akyel female family of Kula-Jal (1916)

152 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kula-Jal, buckskin, foaled 1916. 81 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 2026.

Colours: chestnut 32, bay 31, palomino 22. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 2, 1930s 3, 1940s 3, 1950s 1, 1960s 10, 1970s 10, 1980s 15, 1990s 14, 2000s 18, 2010s 45, 2020s 30.
