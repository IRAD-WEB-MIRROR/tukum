Family C-13 Chongtor female family of Kök-Kush (1919)

8 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kök-Kush, bay, foaled 1919. 2 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1951.

Colours: bay 4, dun 3, black 1. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 1, 1930s 2, 1940s 3, 1950s 1.

The family, mare by mare
