Family A-31 Akyel female family of Kula-Taka (1925)

7 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kula-Taka, chestnut, foaled 1925. 3 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1954.

Colours: chestnut 2, bay 2, cream 2. By decade: 1920s 3, 1930s 2, 1940s 1, 1950s 1.

The family, mare by mare
