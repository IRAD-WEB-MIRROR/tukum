Family C-20 Chongtor female family of Jyluu-Chabysh (1924)

16 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Jyluu-Chabysh, bay, foaled 1924. 10 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1978.

Colours: bay 6, dun 5, grey 3. By decade: 1920s 1, 1930s 1, 1940s 4, 1950s 3, 1960s 3, 1970s 4.
