Family A-15 Akyel female family of Sur-Archa (1920)

10 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Sur-Archa, dun, foaled 1920. 4 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1959.

Colours: chestnut 3, dun 2, golden bay 2. By decade: 1920s 2, 1930s 5, 1940s 2, 1950s 1.
