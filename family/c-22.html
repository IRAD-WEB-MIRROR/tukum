Family C-22 Chongtor female family of Muzdak-Bugu (1924)

91 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Muzdak-Bugu, black, foaled 1924. 44 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 2025.

Colours: bay 38, dun 19, grey 12. By decade: 1920s 3, 1930s 4, 1940s 4, 1950s 10, 1960s 14, 1970s 4, 1980s 7, 1990s 4, 2000s 11, 2010s 18, 2020s 12.
