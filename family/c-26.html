Family C-26 Chongtor female family of Uzun-Suu (1925)

11 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Uzun-Suu, grey, foaled 1925. 6 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1966.

Colours: bay 7, grey 2, red roan 1. By decade: 1920s 1, 1930s 2, 1940s 3, 1950s 4, 1960s 1.
