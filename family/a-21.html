Family A-21 Akyel female family of Kümüsh-Ular (1922)

7 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kümüsh-Ular, bay, foaled 1922. 5 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1950.

Colours: bay 4, grey 1, golden bay 1. By decade: 1920s 1, 1930s 5, 1950s 1.
