Family A-7 Akyel female family of Ak-Kyz (1918)

30 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Ak-Kyz, chestnut, foaled 1918. 14 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1998.

Colours: chestnut 12, bay 10, golden bay 2. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 4, 1930s 2, 1940s 5, 1950s 2, 1960s 10, 1970s 3, 1980s 2, 1990s 1.
