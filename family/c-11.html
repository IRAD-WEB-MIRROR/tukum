Family C-11 Chongtor female family of Mor-Bayge (1918)

9 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Mor-Bayge, dun, foaled 1918. 3 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1947.

Colours: bay 4, dun 2, grey 2. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 1, 1930s 6, 1940s 1.
