Family C-42 Chongtor female family of Tolkun (1946)

11 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Tolkun, grey, foaled 1946. 6 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1988.

Best known: Suuchu.

Colours: dun 4, grey 3, bay 2. By decade: 1940s 1, 1950s 2, 1960s 4, 1970s 2, 1980s 2.
