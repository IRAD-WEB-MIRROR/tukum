Family C-14 Chongtor female family of Keng-Kulan (1920)

8 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Keng-Kulan, dun, foaled 1920. 4 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1948.

Colours: dun 6, grey 1, bay 1. By decade: 1920s 2, 1930s 4, 1940s 2.
