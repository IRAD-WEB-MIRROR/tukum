Family C-23 Chongtor female family of Altyn-Kaiyng (1925)

46 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Altyn-Kaiyng, dun, foaled 1925. 22 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1997.

Colours: bay 12, grey 10, blue roan 7. By decade: 1920s 2, 1930s 2, 1940s 3, 1950s 6, 1960s 12, 1970s 12, 1980s 7, 1990s 2.
