Family A-2 Akyel female family of Kümüsh-Tuyak (1915)

26 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kümüsh-Tuyak, palomino, foaled 1915. 12 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1997.

Colours: chestnut 11, buckskin 4, golden bay 3. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 1, 1930s 6, 1940s 7, 1950s 1, 1960s 4, 1970s 4, 1980s 1, 1990s 1.
