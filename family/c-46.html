Family C-46 Chongtor female family of Jyluu-Tash (1947)

8 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Jyluu-Tash, dun, foaled 1947. 3 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1979.

Colours: dun 2, bay 2, black 1. By decade: 1940s 1, 1950s 2, 1960s 3, 1970s 2.
