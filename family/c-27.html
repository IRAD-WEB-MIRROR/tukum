Family C-27 Chongtor female family of Kula-Yel (1926)

6 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kula-Yel, grey, foaled 1926. 3 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1970.

Colours: blue roan 3, grey 1, dun 1. By decade: 1920s 1, 1930s 1, 1950s 2, 1960s 1, 1970s 1.
