Family C-9 Chongtor female family of Kümüsh-Bugu (1916)

26 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kümüsh-Bugu, bay, foaled 1916. 13 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1968.

Colours: bay 12, grey 6, dun 6. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 2, 1930s 11, 1940s 5, 1950s 4, 1960s 3.
