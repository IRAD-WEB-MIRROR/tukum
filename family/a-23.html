Family A-23 Akyel female family of Ak-Sel (1923)

12 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Ak-Sel, bay, foaled 1923. 5 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1963.

Colours: bay 8, chestnut 2, grey 1. By decade: 1920s 1, 1930s 2, 1940s 4, 1950s 3, 1960s 2.
