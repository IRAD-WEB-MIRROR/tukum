Family C-10 Chongtor female family of Kara-Köknar (1918)

16 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kara-Köknar, bay, foaled 1918. 11 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1976.

Colours: bay 6, dun 5, blue roan 2. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 2, 1930s 4, 1940s 2, 1950s 3, 1960s 3, 1970s 1.
