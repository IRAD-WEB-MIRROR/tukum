Family A-33 Akyel female family of Ak-Möngü (1926)

87 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Ak-Möngü, cream, foaled 1926. 41 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 2026.

Colours: bay 23, chestnut 19, buckskin 14. By decade: 1920s 1, 1930s 1, 1940s 3, 1950s 3, 1960s 11, 1970s 16, 1980s 7, 1990s 5, 2000s 10, 2010s 19, 2020s 11.
