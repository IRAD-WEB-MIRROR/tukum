Family A-14 Akyel female family of Yel-Muz (1919)

5 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Yel-Muz, cream, foaled 1919. 2 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1938.

Colours: cream 2, grey 2, golden bay 1. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 1, 1930s 3.

The family, mare by mare

Each mare's foals are listed under her, oldest first.
