Family C-7 Chongtor female family of Jal-Kush (1915)

10 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Jal-Kush, dun, foaled 1915. 6 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1965.

Colours: dun 3, bay 3, black 1. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 3, 1930s 1, 1940s 2, 1950s 2, 1960s 1.
