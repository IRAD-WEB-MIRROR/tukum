Family A-29 Akyel female family of Uluu-Chabysh (1924)

9 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Uluu-Chabysh, chestnut, foaled 1924. 5 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1963.

Colours: chestnut 3, black 2, bay 2. By decade: 1920s 1, 1930s 1, 1940s 3, 1950s 2, 1960s 2.
