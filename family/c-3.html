Family C-3 Chongtor female family of Kula-Kunduz (1912)

20 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kula-Kunduz, bay, foaled 1912. 15 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1982.

Colours: bay 8, dun 5, grey 3. By decade: 1910s 1, 1920s 1, 1930s 7, 1940s 4, 1950s 5, 1960s 1, 1980s 1.
