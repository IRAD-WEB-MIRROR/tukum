Family C-54 Chongtor female family of Sary-Chiy (1954)

7 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Sary-Chiy, grey, foaled 1954. 3 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1988.

Colours: bay 4, grey 1, dun 1. By decade: 1950s 1, 1960s 2, 1970s 2, 1980s 2.
