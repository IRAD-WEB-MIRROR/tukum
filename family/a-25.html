Family A-25 Akyel female family of Ak-Jaryk (1924)

9 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Ak-Jaryk, grey, foaled 1924. 3 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1958.

Colours: bay 4, chestnut 2, grey 1. By decade: 1920s 2, 1930s 4, 1940s 1, 1950s 2.
