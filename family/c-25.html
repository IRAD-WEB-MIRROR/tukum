Family C-25 Chongtor female family of Kök-Nur (1925)

6 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kök-Nur, grey, foaled 1925. 4 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1980.

Colours: bay 3, grey 2, dun 1. By decade: 1920s 1, 1930s 1, 1940s 1, 1950s 1, 1960s 1, 1980s 1.
