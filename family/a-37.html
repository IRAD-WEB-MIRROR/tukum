Family A-37 Akyel female family of Sary-Alma (1930)

8 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Sary-Alma, palomino, foaled 1930. 5 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1969.

Colours: palomino 4, golden bay 1, chestnut 1. By decade: 1930s 2, 1950s 5, 1960s 1.
