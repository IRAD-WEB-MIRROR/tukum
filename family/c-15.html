Family C-15 Chongtor female family of Kula-Jyldyz (1920)

11 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kula-Jyldyz, bay, foaled 1920. 5 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 1950.

Colours: grey 6, bay 2, mouse-dun 1. By decade: 1920s 3, 1930s 3, 1940s 4, 1950s 1.
