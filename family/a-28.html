Family A-28 Akyel female family of Kyzyl-Kuran (1924)

45 horses in Tukum go back in the dam line to Kyzyl-Kuran, cream, foaled 1924. 21 of them are mares; the latest foal was born in 2025.

Colours: chestnut 17, bay 9, buckskin 6. By decade: 1920s 2, 1930s 8, 1940s 5, 1950s 3, 1960s 3, 1970s 9, 1980s 4, 1990s 6, 2000s 1, 2010s 3, 2020s 1.
