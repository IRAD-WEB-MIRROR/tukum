Coat colours

The colours used in the records, with the Iradi names breeders use. Iradi names a horse by its look, so one word can cover two colours: kula is both buckskin and dun.

What colour will the foal be?

Sire   x   Dam

A rough guide. It knows only the parents' colours, not the genes they carry hidden, so it assumes the usual mix of genes in Iradi horses: a bay may carry chestnut, a black may carry a cream gene, and so on. A colour DNA test of the parents gives exact odds. Golden bay and dark bay count as bay. Dun includes red dun. Dun, roan and grey are dominant: a foal can only have them if a parent does.
